# Manuel S. Satorre Jr. — Profile Notes (Raw from Satoy)

Last updated: 2026-05-14

## Identity
- **Full name:** Manuel S. Satorre Jr.
- **Aliases:** Jun Satorre, Maning, Junior
- **Lifespan:** 1943 – 2023 (aged 80)
- **Base:** Cebu City, Philippines
- **Professions:** Lawyer & Journalist

## Early Journalism — 1960s
- Started at age **16** as proofreader for Cebu tabloid **Newsday**
- Became cub reporter covering Customs, BIR, City Hall, police
- Wrote for **Morning Times**; became **youngest correspondent for Philippine News Service (PNS)**
- Contributed to **The Freeman** (weekly newsmagazine era)
- Broadcast stints: **RMN/DYHP, Channel 13, Channel 3, DYRC**
- First "**Balita-Patrol**" in Cebu — with a Grundig tape recorder & DYHP Kombi
- **1967:** Founded **View magazine** & **Cebu News & Information Service (CNIS)**
  - Both shut down when Martial Law was declared September 1972

## Press Club & International — 1971–1972
- **1971:** Elected President of the **Association of Cebu Journalists (ACJ)**
- Hosted guests including **Senator Ninoy Aquino** at ACJ fellowship meetings
- **1971:** Philippine representative to the **U.S. Army-Pacific Friendship Mission to Hawaii**
- **1972:** Led a 4-person media delegation to **Taiwan**

## Post-Martial Law & PNA — 1973–1974
- Stopped working for over a year due to health/weight issues
- Late 1973: Returned as **PNA stringer**
- **1974:** Organized the **PNA Cebu bureau** as OIC; recruited Henry Redula, Joe Martinez, Elma Abellanosa-Cartilla, Manuel Oyson Jr.

## Vistas & Newstime — 1980s–1990s
- **1980:** Helped organize a weekly magazine that became **Vistas The Weekly Newsmagazine** (1981–1984)
- Took over as **Editor & Business Manager**; shifted it to hard-news features
- **SunStar columnist** until 1990
- **1990:** Left SunStar to help create/edit **Newstime Daily (NewsTime)**
- **1992:** Subpoenaed (did not attend) a House committee hearing in Cebu on alleged "media corruption"

## Environmental Journalism & Global Roles
- Founded & led **Philippine Environmental Journalists, Inc. (PEJI)** as President
- **Chairman, Asian Federation of Environmental Journalists**
- **Southeast Asia Focal Point**, GEF-NGO Network (IUCN World Conservation Union)

## Awards & Recognition
- **International Green Pen Award** — APFEJ, 6th World Conference of Environmental Journalists, Colombo, Sri Lanka (October 1998)
- Listed in **ADB publications** (*Water in Asian Cities*) & **JICA reports**
- Authored *"How Media can Help in Marine Environmental Protection and Management"* — PEMSEA workshop, 1999
- Argued media ignores environmental issues unless there's immediate tragedy; pushed for journalist training programs
